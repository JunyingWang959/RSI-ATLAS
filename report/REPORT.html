What the four measures mean

A0: baseline average. A1: average after round 1. A2: average after round 2.

Ignition I = [(A2 − A1) / (100 − A1)] ÷ [(A1 − A0) / (100 − A0)]. It compares the share of remaining score headroom gained in round 2 with round 1. I > 1 means faster normalized improvement; it does not alone establish recursive self-improvement.
